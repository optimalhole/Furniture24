Furniture Care Guide

Wooden Furniture

Keep wooden surfaces dry and clean them using a soft cloth. Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight and moisture.

Sofas

Vacuum fabric surfaces regularly and immediately clean spills. Follow the care instructions appropriate for the selected fabric.

Dining Furniture

Wipe spills promptly and avoid placing extremely hot objects directly on furniture surfaces.

General Care

Avoid dragging heavy furniture. Lift items when moving them and use suitable protective pads where appropriate.
